title: "Can LDA Outperform QDA When QDA Is Closer to the Truth?"
author: wenhao7
---

Suppose the true class covariance matrices are different, so QDA is closer to the true data-generating process than LDA. Can LDA still have lower test error than QDA when the training sample is small? How does the answer depend on the number of predictors \(p\) relative to the sample size \(n\)?
