When can cross-checking an answer with multiple large language models fail to detect an incorrect result, even when the models agree with each other?

My main concern is that multiple LLMs may not provide truly independent verification. If they share similar training data or reasoning patterns, they may make the same mistake. So I want to understand when model agreement is actually useful evidence and when we need external verification, such as simulations, mathematical reasoning, or primary-source verification.
